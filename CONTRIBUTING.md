# Contributing to Solace

Suggestions and improvements are welcome, and you don't need to be an expert in Solace to offer one.

## Suggesting a change

- **Found a problem or have an idea?** Open an issue and describe what you expected and what happened.
- **Have a fix or an improvement?** Open a pull request. For a large change, an issue first saves you from work that may not fit.
- **Working on the code?** Solace is built with Solace: in the projects listed in the root `solace.toml`, change code through the `solace` CLI rather than editing files directly. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) has the working rules, and the [README](README.md#development) shows how to run the tests.

You're also welcome to fork Solace and extend it under the terms in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). A copy you share must stay free of charge and non-commercial, keep the license and copyright notices, and say that you changed it.
